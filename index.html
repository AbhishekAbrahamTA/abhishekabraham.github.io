Popular Tools for Beginners

You don't need expensive software to start. Here are some widely used editing tools that beginners often choose.

✎
Free

CapCut™

A free video editing application by ByteDance. It offers features such as auto-captions, effects, and templates, and is available on mobile and desktop platforms.

Auto Captions Templates Mobile

★★★★★

Free

DaVinci Resolve™

A video editing application by Blackmagic Design. The free version includes color grading, audio editing, and visual effects tools. It has a steeper learning curve than some alternatives.
